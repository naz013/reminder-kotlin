What Gemini can do in this app

This is a PRO feature. When enabled, Gemini can carry out these actions directly on your device - nothing is sent to any server to do it, and each action still respects your existing reminders, notes and birthdays.

Try asking Gemini things like...

  • "Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow at 9am"
  • "What's on my reminders for the next week?"
  • "Mark my rent reminder as done"
  • "Make a note titled Wi-Fi password with the text ..."
  • "Add my sister's birthday, March 3rd"
  • "Whose birthday is coming up this month?"
  • "Add buy milk to my Google Tasks"
  • "Mark the milk task as done"

Reminders

Create a reminderAction

Creates a new one-time reminder from a title and a due date/time, with optional notes.

List upcoming remindersLookup

Returns active reminders due within a number of days from now (7 by default), soonest first.

Complete a reminderAction

Marks a reminder as done so it stops triggering, without deleting it.

Delete a reminderAction

Permanently removes a reminder.

Notes

Create a noteAction

Creates a new note from a title and body text.

Search notesLookup

Searches your existing notes by title and body text.

Birthdays

Create a birthdayAction

Adds a birthday from a name and date, optionally hiding the birth year.

List upcoming birthdaysLookup

Returns birthdays coming up within a number of days from now (30 by default), soonest first.

Google Tasks

Requires a Google account linked under Google Tasks in this app. If it isn't linked yet, ask Gemini to try again after you sign in.

Create a Google TaskAction

Creates a task in your default Google Tasks list, with optional notes and a due date/time.

List Google TasksLookup

Returns your Google Tasks, excluding already-completed ones unless asked to include them.

Complete a Google TaskAction

Marks a Google Task as done.

Gemini integration on Android is still rolling out and may not be available on every device yet.
