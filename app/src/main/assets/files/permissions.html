Permissions used by this app

Every permission below is requested only for the feature it powers, and most are optional — the app works without granting them, just with that feature turned off.

LocationOptional

Fine, coarse and background location are used to trigger location-based reminders when you enter or leave a place. Your location is never saved or shared.

Call phoneOptional

Lets a reminder place a phone call action you configured. The app never calls without your acceptance.

ContactsOptional

Reading contact birthdays for the Birthdays feature, or getting contact information for a call or message reminder type. Read-only, never uploaded.

CameraOptional

Used for attaching a photo you take to a note.

MicrophoneOptional

Used for voice input when creating a reminder or note.

AccountsOptional

Used for signing in with your Google account to enable Google Drive backup and Google Tasks features.

CalendarOptional

Read/write access is used for storing and getting events from the system or Google calendar.

BiometricOptional

Used for locking the app with your fingerprint or face — this check happens entirely on your device and never leaves it.

Notifications & exact alarmsRequired

Used for showing reminders on time. Without these, the app can't notify you when a reminder is due.

BluetoothOptional

Used only if you set up a reminder trigger or action tied to a Bluetooth device.

See the in-app Privacy Policy for how any data collected through these permissions is used.
